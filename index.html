The Man Behind the Name

Meet Chef Fred "De Mongolian"

A culinary journey that earned a legendary title through authentic excellence and unwavering dedication to the craft.

From Passion to Legacy

Chef Fred's journey into culinary excellence began with a deep passion for creating exceptional dining experiences. With over 15 years of professional experience in Uganda's hospitality industry, he has become a trusted name among top hotels and restaurants.

"The name 'De Mongolian' was earned, not chosen. After creating an authentic Mongolian feast that captivated guests and critics alike, the title stuck. It represents my commitment to authenticity, excellence, and the art of bringing exceptional flavors to life."

Today, Chef Fred De Mongolian Ltd stands as Uganda's premier culinary consultation firm, specializing in kitchen optimization, staff training, menu engineering, and signature Mongolian cuisine. Our mission is to elevate every kitchen we touch to five-star standards.
